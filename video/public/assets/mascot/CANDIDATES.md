# Mascot Candidates

Pixel-art mascot concepts for the Kalshi prediction-markets show (deadpan financial-news
parody). Generated with Gemini `gemini-2.5-flash-image`, then post-processed for pixel
crispness: downscale to 96px longest side (LANCZOS) → quantize to ≤24 colors → upscale 8x
(NEAREST). Each entry has a `<slug>-raw.png` (full-size 1024px model output) and a
processed `<slug>.png`.

House palette: indigo ink `#17122b`, magenta `#c8327a`, teal `#128a72`, pink `#e84581`,
gold `#ffc37a`, cream `#fff3d6`, warm paper `#e9e5dd`.

---

## 1. The Oracle — `oracle.png`

**Prompt:**
> 16-bit pixel art sprite, 'The Oracle', a small hooded pixel seer holding a crystal ball that displays a glowing percent sign (%), mysterious deadpan fortune-teller, front-facing, clean bold silhouette, limited palette of indigo ink #17122b, magenta #c8327a, teal #128a72, pink #e84581, gold #ffc37a, cream #fff3d6, warm paper #e9e5dd, crisp pixels, no anti-aliasing, plain white background, no text watermark, character centered, full body.

**Self-critique:** Strong, compact silhouette with an unmistakable hooded read; magenta
hood trim + indigo + teal ball are dead-on palette. The `%` in the ball is a little
garbled and the vibe reads mystic rather than news-desk — great as a recurring "forecast"
bit character rather than the main anchor.

## 2. The Anchor Goblin — `anchor-goblin.png`

**Prompt:**
> 16-bit pixel art sprite, 'The Anchor Goblin', a green-skinned goblin wearing a slightly-too-big suit and tie, seated at a news anchor desk, deadpan expression, holding a coffee mug with a percent sign (%) printed on it, financial news broadcast, front-facing, clean bold silhouette, limited palette of indigo ink #17122b, magenta #c8327a, teal #128a72, pink #e84581, gold #ffc37a, cream #fff3d6, warm paper #e9e5dd, crisp pixels, no anti-aliasing, plain white background, no text watermark, character centered, full body.

**Self-critique:** Best desk-news vibe of the set — deadpan goblin behind a lit anchor
desk with a ticker line-chart and monitor, exactly the "straight-faced absurd" tone.
Palette skews a touch muted/desaturated (gold and pink underused) and the mug `%` is
faint, but silhouette and staging are excellent. Top pick for the primary host.

## 3. The Quant Owl — `quant-owl.png`

**Prompt:** (retried once; first pass had a garbled background grid instead of a chart and no visible visor)
> 16-bit pixel art sprite, 'The Quant Owl', an owl financial analyst, clearly wearing a bright TEAL GREEN (#128a72) accountant's eyeshade visor on its forehead and a small magenta necktie, one wing gesturing at a large presentation easel showing a bold rising magenta (#c8327a) line chart on a cream board, studious deadpan expression, strong magenta teal and gold color pops, front-facing, clean bold silhouette, limited palette of indigo ink #17122b, magenta #c8327a, teal #128a72, pink #e84581, gold #ffc37a, cream #fff3d6, warm paper #e9e5dd, crisp pixels, no anti-aliasing, plain white background, no text watermark, character centered, full body.

**Self-critique:** Clean owl + easel silhouette with a legible rising magenta line chart —
clear "analyst" role. Palette drifted toward mauve/magenta/cream; the eyeshade visor came
out grey rather than teal, and teal/gold pops are weak. Charming but the least
palette-faithful; would need a color pass.

## 4. CRT Anchorman — `crt-anchorman.png`

**Prompt:**
> 16-bit pixel art sprite, 'CRT Anchorman', a retro boxy robot with an old CRT television for a head, the green screen displays a line chart and the number '51%', wearing a suit collar and tie, deadpan news anchor, front-facing, clean bold silhouette, limited palette of indigo ink #17122b, magenta #c8327a, teal #128a72, pink #e84581, gold #ffc37a, cream #fff3d6, warm paper #e9e5dd, crisp pixels, no anti-aliasing, plain white background, no text watermark, character centered, full body.

**Self-critique:** Bold, iconic silhouette (rabbit-ear CRT head + suited robot body) and the
screen actually renders a line chart plus a legible "51%" — perfect on-brand joke for a
prediction-markets desk. Palette runs dark/indigo-heavy with little magenta or gold pop,
but the concept and readability are the strongest. Co-top pick alongside the Anchor Goblin.
