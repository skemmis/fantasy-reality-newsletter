[beat:cold-open]

Forty-three days.

That is how long the United States government stayed shut last fall — the longest shutdown in the nation's history. And weeks before it ended, a betting market had already told you it would run this long.

The experts on television had not.

[beat:title]

<!-- title card: THE MARKET SAYS · pixel wordmark · ep 03 · "RETRO" kicker -->

[beat:setup]

Quick refresher. October first, twenty-twenty-five: Congress blows the deadline and the government goes dark. The fight — Senate Democrats won't fund the government without extending some expiring health-insurance subsidies; Republicans won't negotiate until it reopens. That funding bill would go on to fail fourteen times.

And every few days, a very serious person explained that a deal was "close." A deal was not close.

[beat:the-bet]

Here's the part that should bother you. While Washington swore the end was days away, traders on Kalshi kept pricing the shutdown to run past forty days. Not pessimism — arithmetic. Nobody was blinking, so the market refused to.

A market, unlike a pundit, cannot be embarrassed. It has no press secretary. It only has a price.

[beat:move-begins]

Day one: furlough notices go out. Roughly forty-two million Americans are warned their food assistance could stop.

[beat:move-record]

November fifth: day thirty-five. The shutdown ties, then breaks, the all-time record. This stunned the cable networks. It did not stun the order book, which had penciled it in weeks earlier.

[beat:meme-react]

[beat:move-ends]

November ninth, the Senate finally blinks — sixty to forty. November twelfth the House agrees, the President signs, the lights come back on. Final count: forty-three days. The market's number, give or take a rounding error.

[beat:so-what]

So next time, who gets your trust — the panel of experts, or the anonymous crowd with money on the line?

Because the crowd is still betting. There is an open market, right now, on whether it all happens again before the end of twenty-twenty-six. The pundits say it won't.

You know where to check.

[beat:endcard]

Resolution Watch. Fall twenty-twenty-five shutdown: resolved YES — forty-three days. The scoreboard doesn't gloat. It just updates.
